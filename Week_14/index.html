ADD 103 STEP-BY-STEP TUTORIAL

How to Change a Car Battery

Follow the eight steps to learn the basic process of replacing a standard 12-volt car battery.

Start Step 1

THE TUTORIAL

Eight steps to follow

01

Gather your tools

Prepare the correct battery and equipment.

02

Prepare the vehicle

Park safely and turn the vehicle off.

03

Locate the battery

Find the battery and identify its terminals.

04

Disconnect the cables

Remove the negative cable before positive.

05

Remove the old battery

Release the hold-down and lift carefully.

06

Install the new battery

Check fit and secure the replacement.

07

Reconnect the cables

Connect positive first, then negative.

08

Check your work

Inspect the installation and test the car.

Safety reminder

Wear eye protection and gloves. Keep flames and sparks away. Check your owner's manual because some vehicles require special procedures. Do not work on a hybrid or electric vehicle's high-voltage battery.
