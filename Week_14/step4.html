STEP 4 OF 8

Disconnect the cables

Progress50%
50%

What to do

For a typical negative-ground 12-volt vehicle, loosen and remove the negative (−) cable first. Move it aside so it cannot touch the terminal. Then remove the positive (+) cable.

Safety note

Do not let a metal tool touch both terminals or connect a terminal to vehicle metal. Follow the exact procedure in your owner's manual.
