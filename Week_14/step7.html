Reconnect the cables

Progress87%
87%

What to do

For a typical negative-ground 12-volt vehicle, reconnect the positive (+) cable first. Tighten it securely, then reconnect the negative (−) cable.

Safety note

Make sure the clamps are secure and correctly positioned. Follow manufacturer torque specifications when provided.
